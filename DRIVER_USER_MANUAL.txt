4. ACCEPTING & MANAGING JOBS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RECEIVING NEW JOBS:

When dispatcher creates a job for you:
  ✅ You get push notification (ding!)
  ✅ Job appears in "Pending Jobs" list
  ✅ Notification shows job type + location
  ✅ You can accept or request reassignment

ACCEPTING A JOB:

Method 1: From Home Screen
  1. See pending job in list
  2. Tap job name
  3. Read full details (address, notes, instructions)
  4. Tap "Accept Job"
  ✅ Job moves to "Active Jobs"
  ✅ Dispatcher gets notification
  ✅ You're ready to navigate

Method 2: From Notification
  1. Tap notification when it arrives
  2. Review job details
  3. Tap "Accept"
  ✅ Immediately navigates to home screen

WHAT'S IN JOB DETAILS:

Every job includes:
  
  📌 LOCATION
     Full address, cross streets, apartment/suite
     Building access code (if needed)
     Special location notes
  
  👤 CUSTOMER
     Name and phone number
     Contact instructions
     Special requests
  
  📝 DELIVERY NOTES
     What you're delivering/picking up
     Special handling (fragile, cold, etc.)
     Signature requirement
     Proof of delivery needed (photo)
  
  ⚡ PRIORITY
     🟢 Normal: Whenever convenient
     🟠 Medium: Within 2 hours preferred
     🔴 High: ASAP, customer waiting
  
  🎯 TYPE
     🚚 Delivery: Bring package to customer
     📦 Pickup: Collect items from location
     🔧 Service: Install/repair/inspect work
     ⚠️  Exception: Special/urgent situation

REJECTING OR DELAYING:

Not available right now?
  1. Tap job
  2. Tap "More" (⋮) menu
  3. Select "Request Reassignment"
  4. Choose reason (too far, too busy, etc.)
  5. Dispatcher reassigns to another driver
  
  ✅ Penalty-free if done before accepting

If You Accept, Then Can't Do It:
  1. Tap active job
  2. Go to "More" menu
  3. Select "Request Help"
  4. Message dispatcher why
  5. Continue with next task (don't abandon)
  
  ⚠️  Multiple rejections may affect pay/standing

VIEWING JOB QUEUE:

Upcoming Jobs List:
  Shows: All jobs assigned to you (in order)
  Sorted: By priority, then by time
  Shows: Address, type, priority level
  
  Tap any job to see full details
  Tap "Accept Next" to work through queue

6. COMPLETING JOBS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

COMPLETING A JOB:

You've arrived! Now what?

Step 1: ARRIVE AT LOCATION
  ✅ You get notification: "You've arrived"
  ✅ System auto-detects (within 50 meters)
  ✅ Build-in safe time window (5-10 min)
  
  Tap: "Mark Arrived" (if auto-detect didn't work)

Step 2: TAKE PROOF PHOTO
  Tap: "Take Photo" button
  
  What to photograph:
    ✓ Package at customer location
    ✓ Customer acceptance/signature
    ✓ Building/address number for reference
    ✓ Damage or issues (if any)
  
  Do NOT:
    ✗ Include customer face in distressing situation
    ✗ Take identifying photos of customer property
    ✗ Upload blurry or dark photos
  
  Guidelines:
    • Well-lit photo (daytime preferred)
    • Shows package/delivery clearly
    • Shows customer acceptance (if signature)
    • Time-stamped automatically

Step 3: GET SIGNATURE (If Required)
  If job requires signature:
    1. Tap "Get Signature"
    2. Customer signs on phone screen
    3. Can add notes ("Customer not home, left at door")
    4. Tap "Confirm"
  
  If customer refuses:
    1. Note: "Signature Declined"
    2. Take photo of refusal
    3. Document reason
    4. Continue (don't force)

Step 4: ADD NOTES (Optional)
  "Any special notes about this delivery?"
  
  Examples:
    ✓ "Left package on back porch per request"
    ✓ "Customer not home - called phone number, no answer"
    ✓ "Building security locked, had to call intercom"
    ✓ "Customer satisfied, gave approval to leave"
    ✓ "Damage to package noted, customer agreed"

Step 5: MARK COMPLETE
  1. Tap "Mark Complete"
  2. Review summary
  3. Tap "Confirm"
  
  ✅ Job completed! Dispatcher notified
  ✅ Next job loads automatically
  ✅ Timestamp recorded

PICKUP JOBS:

Similar to delivery but reversed:
  ✓ Arrive at location
  ✓ Collect items
  ✓ Take photo of items collected
  ✓ Get signature (if required) from person handing over
  ✓ Mark "Complete"

SERVICE JOBS:

For repairs/installations:
  ✓ Arrive at location
  ✓ Document work location (photo)
  ✓ Check: "Work completed as specified?"
  ✓ Take photo of finished work
  ✓ Get approval signature
  ✓ Document time spent
  ✓ Mark "Complete"

EXCEPTION JOBS:

For urgent/unusual:
  ✓ Follow all same steps
  ✓ Document extra details (photos of issues)
  ✓ Communication with dispatcher key
  ✓ Message dispatcher with updates
  ✓ Get confirmation before marking complete

7. LOCATION TRACKING & PRIVACY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

HOW LOCATION TRACKING WORKS:

Your Phone Sends:
  ✓ GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude)
  ✓ Accuracy level (±5-15 meters)
  ✓ Timestamp (when location was recorded)
  ✓ Speed and heading (direction)
  
  Sent to: Company servers continuously
  Frequency: Every 5 seconds when app active
  When offline: Saved locally, synced when reconnected

Why We Track:
  ✓ Route optimization (avoiding traffic)
  ✓ ETA calculations (accurate arrival times)
  ✓ Job verification (proof you visited location)
  ✓ Safety (can locate you if emergency)
  ✓ Performance analytics (for bonuses)

Who Can See Your Location:
  • Your dispatcher (always)
  • Your manager (always)
  • You (your own history in app)
  • Emergency dispatch (if accident/emergency)
  • NOT: Other drivers, customers, public

PRIVACY SETTINGS:

App-Level:
  Settings → Location Services
  
  Options:
    ├─ While Using App ✅ RECOMMENDED
    │  (GPS on when app open, off when closed)
    │
    ├─ Always Allow ⚠️  (Battery drain)
    │  (Tracks you 24/7, even at home)
    │
    └─ Never ❌ (Won't work)
       (No tracking, can't complete jobs)

Phone-Level (iOS):
  Settings → Privacy → Location Services
  → Delivery Intelligence
  → Select "While Using the App"

Phone-Level (Android):
  Settings → Apps & Notifications
  → Delivery Intelligence
  → Permissions → Location
  → Select "Allow only while using the app"

DATA DELETION:

Your location history:
  • Saved for 30 days
  • Automatically deleted after
  • Can manually request deletion
  • Backed up for 90 days (legal requirement)

BATTERY IMPACT:

GPS tracking uses battery. Impact:
  • ~5-10% per hour of driving
  • ~2-3% per hour while stationary
  • Varies by phone and terrain
  
  Tips to reduce battery drain:
    ✓ Reduce screen brightness
    ✓ Close other apps
    ✓ Enable Battery Saver mode
    ✓ Plug in while driving (USB car charger)
    ✓ Disable Bluetooth if not used

LOCATION ACCURACY:

Where you are most accurate:
  • Open areas (parking lots, streets): ±5 meters
  • Suburban areas: ±10 meters
  • Urban canyon (tall buildings): ±20-50 meters
  • Indoors: ±20-100 meters
  • Tunnels: No GPS signal

If location looks wrong:
  • Step outside building
  • Wait 10 seconds for GPS lock
  • Accuracy will improve
  • Don't ignore this; inaccuracy affects pay

8. OFFLINE MODE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT IS OFFLINE MODE?

When you lose internet connection (no WiFi, no cellular):

Status Bar Shows:
  🔴 "⊘ Offline (will reconnect)"

What Still Works:
  ✅ View active job details
  ✅ Navigation to location
  ✅ Complete job (mark complete, photo, signature)
  ✅ Work on your queue without interruption

What's Queued Locally:
  ✅ All your completed deliveries
  ✅ Photos taken
  ✅ Signatures captured
  ✅ Your GPS location (up to 500 updates)

When You Reconnect:
   1. Status bar turns 🟠 Orange ("Reconnecting...")
   2. All queued data uploads automatically
   3. Becomes 🟢 Green ("Location Tracking Active")
   4. Takes 10-30 seconds typically

USING OFFLINE MODE EFFECTIVELY:

Best Practice:
  1. Accept multiple jobs before driving
  2. Download offline maps
  3. Note addresses before connection lost
  4. Complete jobs normally (all functions work)
  5. Connection lost? Keep working!
  6. Data auto-syncs when reconnected
  7. Check status bar confirms green
  8. Continue with next job

Offline Duration:
  • Short gap (< 1 hour): No problem
  • Longer gaps (> 2 hours): Notify dispatcher
  • Underground/tunnels: Expected, normal
  • Persistent offline: Check settings → airplane mode off

What NOT to Do Offline:
  ✗ Disable location services (defeats purpose)
  ✗ Expect to accept new jobs (no network)
  ✗ Try to call dispatcher (no signal)
  ✗ Leave app in background (wastes data)
  ✗ Panic if status shows red (it's normal)

RECOVERING FROM OFFLINE:

If stuck reconnecting:
  1. Move to area with better signal
  2. Restart the app
  3. Restart your phone
  4. Check WiFi is enabled
  5. Wait 30 seconds
  6. Should reconnect automatically
  7. Message dispatcher if persists

10. TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

COMMON ISSUES:

Issue: Status bar shows 🔴 Red "Offline"
Cause: No internet connection
Solutions:
  1. Check WiFi is enabled (but don't rely on it while driving)
  2. Check cellular signal strength
  3. Move to area with better signal
  4. Restart app (close and reopen)
  5. Restart phone if persists
  6. Work offline if necessary (data will sync)

Issue: Status bar shows 🟠 Orange "Connecting..." for long time
Cause: Temporary network issue
Solutions:
  1. Wait 30 seconds (usually resolves)
  2. Check internet connection
  3. Toggle airplane mode off/on
  4. Restart app
  5. If > 5 minutes: Restart phone
  → Will eventually reconnect (don't panic)

Issue: Status bar shows 🟣 Purple "GPS Permission Required"
Cause: App doesn't have location permission
Solutions:
  1. Tap status bar → opens settings
  2. Enable location services
  3. Set to "While Using the App"
  4. Return to app
  5. Status should turn 🟢 Green

  iPhone:
    Settings → Privacy → Location Services
    → Delivery Intelligence → While Using the App
  
  Android:
    Settings → Apps → Delivery Intelligence
    → Permissions → Location → Allow while using the app

Issue: Not receiving new job notifications
Cause: Notification settings disabled
Solutions:
  1. Settings → Notifications
  2. Enable "Job Notifications"
  3. Enable "Sound"
  4. Check system settings (phone-level)
  
  iPhone:
    Settings → Notifications → Delivery Intelligence
    → Allow Notifications = ON
    → Sounds = ON
  
  Android:
    Settings → Apps → Delivery Intelligence
    → Notifications → All job notifications enabled

Issue: Photos not uploading / stuck in "Processing"
Cause: Photo too large or poor connection
Solutions:
  1. Wait 5 minutes (try uploading in background)
  2. Move to area with better connection
  3. Reduce photo quality: Settings → Photo Quality
  4. Restart app
  5. If still stuck: Manually retry from job details

Issue: GPS location is inaccurate (shows wrong street)
Cause: Poor GPS signal or indoor location
Solutions:
  1. Step outside building
  2. Wait 10 seconds for GPS lock
  3. Accuracy should improve
  4. If still wrong: Manually pin location on map
  5. Message dispatcher with correction

Issue: Battery draining too fast
Cause: GPS tracking uses battery, screen on
Solutions:
  1. Reduce screen brightness
  2. Disable Bluetooth if not needed
  3. Disable WiFi while driving (just use cellular)
  4. Close other apps
  5. Enable Battery Saver mode
  6. Use car charger (recommended for 8+ hour shifts)

Issue: App crashes or freezes
Cause: Software glitch or memory issue
Solutions:
  1. Force close app (swipe away, force close)
  2. Wait 10 seconds
  3. Reopen app
  4. If persists: Restart phone
  5. If still happens: Reinstall app from store

Issue: Can't login / "Invalid credentials"
Cause: Wrong password or account issue
Solutions:
  1. Double-check username/password (case-sensitive)
  2. Check CAPS LOCK is off
  3. Clear app cache: Settings → Storage → Clear Cache
  4. Try logout, then login again
  5. Contact support if still failing

Issue: Dispatcher says I'm being tracked wrong / false location
Cause: GPS inaccuracy or delayed sync
Solutions:
  1. Confirm status bar is 🟢 Green
  2. Step outside (if indoors)
  3. Wait 10 seconds for update
  4. Tap job "Refresh Location" button
  5. Message dispatcher: "Location syncing now"
  6. In 30 seconds, accuracy improves

11. FAQ
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: How much data does this app use?
A: Approximately 1-2 MB per hour of use
   GPS + messaging + photos
   Recommend: Unlimited data plan or 5GB/month minimum
   If limited data: Use WiFi at home/breaks

Q: Will this app track me after work hours?
A: No. Location tracking only while app is open.
   Set to "While Using the App" in permissions
   We see no location when app is closed
   After you clock out, you're invisible to system

Q: What if I forget to turn on location services?
A: App won't work. You'll see purple status bar.
   Tap it → automatically opens settings
   Enable location → return to app
   Jobs won't track without location enabled

Q: Can I use Apple Maps or Google Maps instead?
A: Yes! We support "Open in Maps" button
   Your choice of app
   Tracking still works in background
   Company sees your location regardless

Q: What if customer isn't home for delivery?
A: Follow the procedure:
   1. Try buzzer/doorbell (wait 2 minutes)
   2. Call customer phone number (if provided)
   3. Leave message with reason
   4. Take photo of building/door
   5. Ask neighbor (optional, if safe)
   6. Mark "Incomplete" with reason
   7. Message dispatcher immediately
   8. DO NOT leave at door unauthorized
   Dispatcher will follow up with customer

Q: Can I reject a job after accepting?
A: Yes, but it may affect standing:
   1. Tap active job
   2. Tap "More" menu
   3. "Request Help" or "Report Issue"
   4. Message dispatcher reason
   5. Explain why can't complete
   6. Wait for reassignment
   If multiple rejections: Consequences possible

Q: What counts as completed?
A: Job marked "Complete" when:
   ✓ You arrived at location (GPS confirmed)
   ✓ Photo taken (proof of delivery/pickup)
   ✓ Signature collected (if required)
   ✓ Notes added (if special situation)
   ✓ Final status confirmed
   Time: 5 minutes typical per job

Q: How are my daily earnings calculated?
A: Based on:
   ✓ Jobs completed ($ per job)
   ✓ On-time percentage (completion rate)
   ✓ Customer ratings (if applicable)
   ✓ Bonus multipliers (performance)
   Check: Settings → Earnings for breakdown

Q: What if my phone dies mid-delivery?
A: Offline mode protects you:
   ✓ Jobs completed while offline are queued
   ✓ When powered back on, they upload
   ✓ Dispatcher sees you completed them
   ✓ Still get paid
   
   Bring charger, avoid this situation!

Q: Can dispatcher see my personal photos/contacts?
A: No. App only accesses:
   ✓ Delivery photos (app-specific)
   ✓ Your location (GPS coordinates only)
   ✓ Your GPS location history
   ✗ Cannot access photos library
   ✗ Cannot access contacts
   ✗ Cannot access messages/calls
   
   Only what app uses for work

Q: What happens if I disable location services?
A: App will not work:
   ✓ Status bar shows purple "GPS Required"
   ✗ Cannot complete jobs
   ✗ Cannot be tracked
   ✗ Cannot claim jobs were completed
   ✗ Won't get paid
   
   Must keep enabled during work hours

Q: How long does photo upload take?
A: Typically:
   • Good signal: < 5 seconds
   • OK signal: 5-30 seconds  
   • Poor signal: 30+ seconds (queued)
   • Offline: Queued, syncs when online
   
   Don't worry if "Processing" shows for time
   Will complete automatically

Q: Can I work for multiple companies?
A: Not simultaneously with app tracking:
   ✓ Can clock out, close app, use personal delivery app
   ✓ But once logged in: Company sees your location
   ✓ Should not:
     - Log in for one company while working for another
     - Have multiple driver accounts active
     - Share credentials (account tied to you)

Q: What is "Bonus Multiplier"?
A: Earning acceleration based on performance:
   • Complete jobs on time → 1.1x pay
   • Accept urgent jobs → 1.2x multiplier
   • High customer ratings → 1.15x bonus
   • Excellent safety record → 1.1x bonus
   
   Stacked: Can reach 1.5x+ pay! 
   See details in app: Earnings section

Q: Who is my dispatcher?
A: The person who assigns your jobs

GLOSSARY OF TERMS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

API: Application Programming Interface
     The system that connects app to company servers

Cellular: Mobile data network (4G/5G)
          What you use when not on WiFi

GPS: Global Positioning System
     Technology that finds your exact location using satellites

Job: A delivery, pickup, service visit, or special task

Offline: No internet connection
         But app still works, syncs when reconnected

POD: Proof of Delivery
     Photo + signature confirming job completed

Queue: List of jobs waiting to be done
       In order of priority

Push Notification: Message that pops up on your phone
                   Usually with sound

Status: Current state of your app/location tracking
        Green = Good, Red = Problem, Orange = Checking

Sync: Upload of offline data when reconnected
      Automatic, takes 10-30 seconds typically

Timestamp: Exact date/time something happened
           Recorded automatically by app

WiFi: Wireless internet network
      Good for stationary work, avoid while driving
