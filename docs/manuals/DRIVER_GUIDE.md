# Driver Guide — Delivery Intelligence Platform (DIP)

One page for your day on the road. Questions → call dispatch.

## Before your shift

1. Open the **DIP app** on your phone and log in with the username and password dispatch gave you.
2. Allow **Location**, **Camera** and **Notifications** when the phone asks. Without them the app can't record proof or alert you.
3. Keep the app open while you work. It checks for new work every 30 seconds.

## How you know there is work

- 🔔 You get a notification: **"New delivery"**, **"New pickup & delivery"** or **"Ready for pickup"**. Tap it to open your stop list.
- Or pull down on **Home** or **Manifest** to refresh.

## Your stops (Manifest tab)

Each stop shows what to do **next**:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PICK UP** · ● Ready | Go to the pickup address and collect the package |
| **PICK UP** · ○ Not ready yet | Don't go yet; you'll get a "Ready for pickup" alert |
| **DELIVER** | The package is with you; take it to the delivery address |
| **DELIVERED** / **PROBLEM** | Finished |

Always read the **amber ⚠ instructions** (e.g. *"Leave with concierge"*, *"Ask for Luis at the parts counter"*) and the **suite / floor / dock**.

## At the pickup

1. Tap **Directions** to navigate there.
2. Collect the package, then tap **Picked up** (or scan its label on the **Scanner** tab).
3. The stop switches to **DELIVER**.

## At the delivery

1. Tap **Directions**, then **Deliver** when you arrive.
2. **Take a photo** of the package at the door/desk (required, in focus, with light).
3. Ask the recipient to **sign** on the screen (when possible).
4. Tap **Confirm**. The time, your GPS position, photo and signature are saved as proof.
   - *"Retake Photo"* → the photo was too dark/blurry; take another.
   - *"Saved Offline"* → no signal; it sends by itself when you're back online. Don't redo it.

## When you can't complete a stop

Tap **Problem** (Manifest) or **Report problem** (deliver screen), pick the reason (closed, refused, wrong address, no access, not ready at pickup, damaged, other) and add a note. Dispatch sees it immediately and will tell you what to do next. **Don't leave the package** unless dispatch says so.

## Good to know

- **Proofs** tab: everything you've delivered, with photo and signature.
- "**Session expired**" → log in again; nothing is lost.
- "**Not your package**" → it's assigned to another driver; call dispatch.
- Battery saver can stop GPS. Keep the phone charged and the app open.
